# Table Crm_Competitor_Product_Prices


## Entity

Entity: [Crm.Marketing.CompetitorProductPrices](~/entities/Crm.Marketing.CompetitorProductPrices.md)

Contains observed prices of products offered by competitors and links them to the corresponding company products. Entity: Crm_Competitor_Product_Prices (Introduced in version 27.1.1.52)

## Summary

| Name | Type | Description |
| - | - | --- |
|[Competitor_Id](#competitor_id)|`uniqueidentifier` |Competitor offering the product.|
|[Competitor_Product_Code](#competitor_product_code)|`nvarchar(64)` |Competitor’s own SKU / product code, if known.|
|[Competitor_Product_Name](#competitor_product_name)|`nvarchar(254)` `ML`|Competitor’s product name as advertised.|
|[Competitor_Product_Price_Id](#competitor_product_price_id)|`uniqueidentifier` `PK`||
|[Currency_Id](#currency_id)|`uniqueidentifier` |Currency of the price.|
|[Notes](#notes)|`nvarchar(max)` |Additional information.|
|[Observed_On](#observed_on)|`date` |Date on which this price was observed.|
|[Price](#price)|`decimal(13, 5)` |Observed competitor price.|
|[Price_Quantity](#price_quantity)|`decimal(10, 3)` |Quantity to which the price applies. By default it applies to 1.|
|[Price_Quantity_Measurement_Unit_Id](#price_quantity_measurement_unit_id)|`uniqueidentifier` |The measurement unit of the quantity to which the price applies.|
|[Product_Id](#product_id)|`uniqueidentifier` |Our product against which the competitor product is compared.|
|[Row_Version](#row_version)|`timestamp` ||
|[Source](#source)|`nvarchar(500)` |Where the price came from, e.g. website, catalogue, sales representative.|
