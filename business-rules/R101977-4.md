# R101977-4 Service Profile - Location Belongs To Enterprise Company

+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Code**             | R101977-4                                                                                          |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Entity**           | @Crm.Pos.ServiceProfiles                                                                           |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Name**             | LocationBelongsToEnterpriseCompany                                                                   |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Attribute**        | EnterpriseCompany, Location                                                                        |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Layer**            | Back-End                                                                                           |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Events**           | Commit                                                                                             |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Record State**     | Inserted, Updated                                                                                  |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Priority**         | Normal                                                                                             |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Modify**           | NO                                                                                                 |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Applicable         | [ALL // no condition needed](xref:applicable-legislations)                                         |
| Legislations**       |                                                                                                    |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Action**           | Validate that the service profile location belongs to the specified enterprise company.            |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Description**      | When both EnterpriseCompany and Location are specified, the location must belong to the selected    |
|                      | enterprise company. If either is NULL, validation succeeds.                                        |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Message**          | The service profile location must belong to the specified enterprise company.                      |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Version**          | Introduced: v.27.1                                                                                 |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Revocable**        | NO                                                                                                 |
