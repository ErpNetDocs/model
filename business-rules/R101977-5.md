| **Entity**           | @Crm.Pos.ServiceProfiles                                                                           |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Attribute**        | LocationGroup, Location                                                                            |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Layer**            | Back-End                                                                                           |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Events**           | Commit                                                                                             |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Record State**     | Inserted, Updated                                                                                  |
+----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Priority**         | Normal                                                                                             |
